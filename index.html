Engineered for Privacy & Scale

A personal media manager that treats your privacy as a non-negotiable architectural invariant.

Multi-Account Storage Pooling

Connect multiple Google Drive accounts. PhotoVault's intelligent allocator fills accounts sequentially with built-in safety margins, giving you one unified photo library.

Local-First & Zero Servers

No intermediary databases or third-party web services. Your media catalog lives in an encrypted SQLite database on your device, talking directly to Google Drive via official APIs.

Thumbnail-First Performance

Optimized image caching and thumbnail pipelines ensure buttery smooth 120Hz scrolling through tens of thousands of media items without downloading heavy raw originals.

Resumable Background Uploads

Powered by Android WorkManager. Uploads are chunked, durable, idempotent, and survive process death, network switches, or battery constraints.

Instant Disaster Recovery

PhotoVault writes an encrypted manifest to your storage accounts. If you switch devices or reinstall, you can reconstruct your entire media library in seconds.

Hardware Keystore Protection

All authentication tokens are secured with Android EncryptedSharedPreferences backed by hardware Keystore keys. No plaintext tokens, ever.

Google OAuth & Data Protection

Direct Peer-to-Cloud Architecture

PhotoVault operates on a zero-telemetry, local-first paradigm. When you connect a Google account, your Android device communicates directly with Google's Drive API endpoints.

  • ✓
    Restricted & Minimal Scopes: We only request https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. PhotoVault cannot see or read your unrelated personal Drive documents.
  • ✓
    Zero Intermediary Servers: Your media, tokens, and metadata are never sent to any third-party server or developer-owned backend.
  • ✓
    Google User Data Policy Compliance: PhotoVault strictly complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

Data Privacy Guarantees

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PhotoVault storage, security, and permissions.

Why does PhotoVault ask for Google Drive permissions?
PhotoVault uses Google Drive as a decentralized, personal cloud backend for your photos and videos. We request access to create, upload, synchronize, and retrieve media inside your own dedicated PhotoVault folders across your connected accounts.
Can PhotoVault view my other personal Google Drive files?
No. PhotoVault uses the scoped permission drive.file which only grants access to files and folders that are created or opened specifically with PhotoVault. The app cannot read, view, or modify your existing spreadsheets, documents, or unrelated files in Google Drive.
Are my photos uploaded to a third-party server?
Never. PhotoVault has no intermediate backend servers, databases, or cloud proxies. All data transfers occur directly between your device and Google's official Drive servers using encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.
How does multi-account pooling work?
You can link multiple Google accounts (for example, two 15 GB free accounts). PhotoVault's storage engine treats them as a single combined pool (e.g. 30 GB), sequentially allocating uploads while preserving individual file integrity and leaving safety margins.
How do I delete my data or disconnect?
You can disconnect any Google account at any time in the app settings, which immediately purges all local OAuth tokens and cache from your device. You can also delete media directly in the app or delete the PhotoVault folder from your Google Drive.
